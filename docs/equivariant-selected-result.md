## Complete policy and quality results

All fourteen completed policies pass fresh independent physical numeric replay
and the unchanged compact/v3 exact certificate. The largest numeric metric gap
is `1.1102230246251565e-16`, within the declared `1e-10` comparison tolerance.
Production solving, both trainers, exact/dyadic constructors and callbacks,
production ranking/pair/world paths, all three native trainers and subprocess
execution are forbidden during independent replay. Verifier hand/board/world,
state/row/action, bit and exact-work caps remain unchanged.

The symmetric c/d exchange moves actual private holdings as well as public
reveals. Every action probability matches its mapped row bitwise in all four new
reference samples. Both reductions also repeat identical same-runtime policies
across their three untraced calls and traced call. Default-versus-new complete
policies match in this small symmetric case, the weight-broken asymmetric case
and one-world T1000 control. This does not imply general trajectory identity.

On the original 22-world game, the largest action-probability difference is
**0.5**, while the largest difference among the eight reported metrics is
`1.6653345369377348e-16`. Both full profiles and their independent best responses
are retained. Small aggregate metric differences do not establish equivalence
of the affected information rows or make them safe to discard. The new reduction
is explicit, and the default retains its original trajectory.

Twelve T10 certificates remain misses. Symmetric T10 exact normalized NashConv
is approximately `0.00227272727272727` BB. Original 22-world T10 is approximately
`0.657530261161674` BB in either mode, with distinct exact rational results.
Only the two one-world T1000 calls pass the unchanged `1/1000`-BB target, at about
`1.66881679796709e-5` BB. The original T1000 request rejects before ranking or
training, in 0.092712 seconds; every instrumented work-entry counter is zero.
This remains an admission rejection, not a failed trained policy.
