## Profiles and next computational boundary

Two separate fresh original-22 T10 profiles authenticate the same source before
and after, retain full policies, and verify their complete strategy/eight metrics
against the corresponding formal saved samples. Their archive SHA256 is
`82c829107d0d1d8e8eb8ba80ba336945302683907fea9e607160417f10900c2b`.
Profiler durations are instrumentation, not additional benchmark samples or
proof of causal speedups.

The expanded reference records 14,741,418 calls; the quotient records 22,567,882.
Both execute 16,215 exact terminal/dyadic preflights. Q additionally executes
38,400 full-history decision-route validations and 38,400 suit-history transforms;
string-prefix and revealed-board parsing are prominent. This supports removing
repeated authenticated-history work and planning native exact computation as
separate measured follow-ups. It does not establish that either change will
improve the complete solve.

Existing native DAG helpers implement legacy binary64 probability/reduction
protocols. They cannot be relabeled as this exact-product/Q backend. A new native
boundary must preserve global exact normalization, bounded integer arithmetic,
correct completed rational rounding and strict adapter rejection, sorted chance
and fixed action arithmetic, full row-member coverage, and saved independent
certification. Native exact computation remains unresolved.

## Controls, failures and remaining gates

The 71-test quotient/existing solver/storage selection passes optimized, and the
final 21 protocol/whole-game controls pass optimized after adding private-payoff
and tiny-prior cases. Ten plan and eighteen graph controls cover member masks,
weights, graph closure, cycles, low remaining budgets, immutability, ownership
and setup failure. Nine independent Q controls rerun on frozen source cover
duplicate/missing/unequal responder and average updates, menus, actual Q arrays
and GC-disabled retained-traceback cleanup. Earlier production defects and
reviewer-only frozenset/message fixture failures remain preserved.

A pre-freeze real-child smoke exposed a runner-only card-list/string comparison
hidden by fake records. Its diagnostic is saved; the original raw child and
pre-fix runner were not retained. A hash-mismatching attempted reconstruction was
rejected. Real child validation and protected replay pass after repair, before
the first formal batch. This evidence gap must not be described as preserved
raw failure data.

Final publication CI remains separate. Higher-order/asymmetric storage,
compact strategy query artifacts, scalable independent certification, native
exact computation, broader convergence and unrestricted full-deck training
remain unresolved. The overall foundation goal stays active.
