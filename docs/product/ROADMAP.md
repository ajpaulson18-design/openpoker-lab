## Commercial strategy: established versus unknown

Established: commercial success in the product's niche matters; accessibility,
contextual education and opponent-aware transparent analysis are differentiation
directions. Reliability, honest scope and reproducibility are part of customer
value. The owner's personal-use benchmark guides quality.

Unknown: target paid segment, pricing, subscriptions versus purchase, hosting,
distribution, support costs, traction, retention, revenue forecasts and competitor
ranking. No market research or financial projections were performed in this
knowledge audit. [Research records](../solver-research.md) concern algorithm design
and provenance, not product-market fit. Do not convert them into market evidence.

The application currently binds to loopback. Public commercial hosting would need
a separately approved security and operational design, not simply a bind change.
Current MIT/public status is preserved; commercial intent alone does not approve
a license or visibility change. [License boundaries](../research/LICENSING.md).
