# Planned blocker-sensitive AK/KK count sources

The [rank-free planner](../scripts/preflop_ak_kk_basis_plan.py) proposes three
new physical count sources: suited `KcAc` versus `KdKh`, and offsuit `KcAd`
versus `KdKh` and `KhKs`. These are three distinct joint global suit orbits.
No AK counts, equity table or strategy result has been generated by this plan.

This extends the [JJ source-count method](preflop-jj-kk-basis-results.md) toward
unpaired hands that share a king rank with the opponent. A future weighted
AA/AK-versus-KK game has 22 SB hands and six BB hands: 132 raw candidates,
48 blocked pairs and 84 compatible pairs. Each compatible pair has prior
1/84 under unit hand weights. AA, AK suited and AK offsuit have masses 3/7,
1/7 and 3/7. The six total suit classes comprise the three existing AA sources
and three new AK sources. Count agreement and independent transport proof
remain prerequisites for using these classes.

## Full future count schedule

The complete literal request is validated before any source report, compiler,
build, executable or output lookup. Ordinary reference/native four-pair caps
are unchanged. The future experiment must perform all-five current-build
conformance and published AA/KK calibration before its AK queries:

| Request | SB hand | BB hands | Compatible pairs |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| AA calibration | AdAs | KdKs, KcKd, KcKh | 3 |
| AK suited | KcAc | KdKh | 1 |
| AK offsuit | KcAd | KdKh, KhKs | 2 |

Both independent best-five reference and native counters must execute all
three requests. Including the all-five dump, the schedule has seven numerical
subprocesses. Each method covers 10,273,824 boards and 20,547,648 seven-card
calls; the reference also visits 431,500,608 five-card subsets. The conformance
dump has 2,598,960 six-byte tuples, or 15,593,760 bytes, retained outside Git.
These named operation and staging counts are no time, CPU or RSS guarantee.

The AA stage is calibration against existing published evidence, not a new
source result. Source and build identities, all raw outputs and errors, and
every failed gate must be preserved. Neither an admitted plan nor native/reference
agreement alone substitutes for independently authenticated physical evidence.

## Boundaries and status

The separate prepared-pool cap remains 128 compatible pairs. AA/AK's 84 pairs
fit it; combining all AA/QQ/JJ/AK hands against KK would require 156 and remains
outside that cap. No larger range admission is implied. Three-rank pocket-pair
proofs do not establish blocker-sensitive strategy behavior.

The [planner tests](../tests/test_preflop_ak_kk_basis_plan.py) check whole-request
admission, cap preservation, exact priors and complete suit-orbit coverage with
ranking, executable lookup and subprocesses forbidden. A frozen numerical
runner, actual observation, independent saved authentication, policy evidence
and GitHub publication checks remain future work. Existing shared kernels,
later-street APIs, production defaults and Coach are unchanged.

This is original project planning based on literal card combinations and global
suit bijections. No third-party implementation or dependency is incorporated.
